Read [the shared workflow](../../../docs/source-model-inspection/README.md).

Use the verified original model and complete native-model checkpoint. Preserve
the entire recovered scene, materials, original native levels and camera units.
Use the scene exporter and Blender renderer supplied by the workflow; retain
their hashes/counts/receipts and inspect both approach sides plus the actual
threshold or landing. Reuse the scene and selected camera views during iteration.

Match visual findings to original source IDs, persisted membership, room
identities, full-width support, source material, access and continuous clearance.
Use the local repair loop and keep unknown/held links disabled. A render,
oriented bound, sampled ray or graph witness never independently qualifies a
physical route. Source-model renders must be labelled separately from deployed
viewer screenshots and on-site evidence.

For UNBC, continue from the October 16 handoffs and latest October 18 door
threshold report before using historical scratch paths or snapshots. The failed
prepared rebuild is diagnostic, not an accepted master. Pair exact source and
evidence in the portable backup without duplicating the large native inputs.
